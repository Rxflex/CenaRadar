# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming and harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, sexual
orientation, disability, neurodivergence, physical appearance, body size,
age, race, nationality, primary language, or technology choices.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming. Assume good faith.
- Be considerate. Your work will be read and critiqued by others.
- Be respectful. Disagreement is fine; personal attacks are not.
- Choose your words carefully. Be kind to others.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment of any kind — verbal, physical, or in writing.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
  (doxxing).
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in a warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban from the
project at the discretion of the maintainers.

To report a violation, open a private issue or contact a maintainer directly.
All reports are reviewed confidentially.
